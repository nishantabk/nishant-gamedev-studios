YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 23 November 2026

Last Updated: 23 November 2026

Nishant GameDev Studios ("we", "our", "us", or "Studio") respects the privacy of our players, website visitors, and users.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you visit our website or play our games.

This policy is intended to cover our games and services across supported platforms, including mobile devices, computers, and other gaming platforms.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Our official website.
  • Our video games and applications.
  • Online game services and multiplayer features.
  • Player support and communication services.
  • Other services operated by our Studio.

Some games may have additional privacy notices depending on their features, platform, or technical requirements.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the game or service you use, we may collect or receive the following categories of information.

2.1 Personal Information

If you voluntarily create an account, contact us, or use certain online features, we may receive:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account identifier.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Messages sent to our support team.

Not every game requires an account or collects personal information.

2.2 Device Information

Our games or service providers may collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device type and model.
  • Operating system and version.
  • Game version.
  • Screen resolution.
  • Language and region settings.
  • Device identifiers where permitted.
  • Network connection information.

2.3 Gameplay Information

To provide and improve gameplay, certain games may process information such as:

  • Game progress and saved game data.
  • Completed levels and achievements.
  • In-game settings and preferences.
  • Gameplay statistics and scores.
  • Multiplayer activity where applicable.
  • Game performance and error information.

2.4 Diagnostics and Crash Reports

We or our service providers may receive technical reports when a game crashes, freezes, or experiences an error.

These reports may contain game version, device information, error logs, and performance information.

2.5 Payment Information

Some games may offer paid downloads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or other digital products.

We do not collect or store complete payment card details through our games. Payments are processed by the relevant platform or payment provider.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless a particular game feature clearly requires it and appropriate legal requirements are met, we do not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as:

  • Government identification numbers.
  • Personal financial account credentials.
  • Precise location information.
  • Biometric information.
  • Health information.

The actual information collected depends on the features and permissions of each game.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing and operating our games.
  • Saving and restoring game progress.
  • Providing online and multiplayer features.
  • Responding to player support requests.
  • Fixing bugs and technical problems.
  • Improving gameplay and user experience.
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse.
  • Processing purchases through platform providers.
  • Understanding general game performance.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

5. Offline and Online Gameplay

5.1 Offline Games

Games designed for offline use may store game progress, settings, and other information locally on your device.

Local data may be lost if you uninstall the game, reset your device, or delete its application data.

5.2 Online Games

Games using online services may process information through servers or third-party service providers to support accounts, multiplayer sessions, cloud saves, and other online functionality.

6. Advertising and Marketing

Some games may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers. The information collected by advertising providers may vary depending on the advertising provider, platform, game features, and user settings.

Depending on the advertising service and applicable settings or laws, these providers may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device and application information.
  • Advertisement interaction information.
  • General usage information.

Advertising providers may use this information to deliver, measure, or improve advertisements.

Where required, consent and advertising preferences will be handled according to applicable laws and platform requirements.

7. Analytics and Performance Services

We may use analytics or performance monitoring services to understand how our games work.

These services may help us understand:

  • Game crashes and technical errors.
  • Loading times and performance issues.
  • General gameplay activity.
  • Feature usage and stability.

Such services may process technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Third-Party Services

Our games and website may use third-party services to provide certain features.

These may include:

  • App stores and distribution platforms.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Advertising networks.
  • Analytics providers.
  • Cloud hosting and storage providers.
  • Authentication and account services.
  • Crash reporting and technical services.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies. The specific third-party services used may vary between games and services.

9. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our games, maintain services, process transactions, or provide requested features.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and safety of users and our services.

10. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The type of information.
  • The purpose for which it was collected.
  • Account and service requirements.
  • Legal and security obligations.

Locally stored game data may remain on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or software service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Our games and services may be used by people of different age groups. However, the information practices of each game must follow applicable age requirements and laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other authorization is required without obtaining the required authorization.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

13. Multiplayer and Community Features

Some games may include multiplayer modes, leaderboards, chat, player profiles, or community features.

Information such as display names, scores, achievements, or messages may be visible to other players depending on the feature.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information through public or multiplayer features.

14. Cloud Saves and Account Services

Certain games may offer cloud saving or account synchronization.

If enabled, game progress and related information may be stored or processed by a cloud service provider.

Availability of cloud saves may depend on the platform, account settings, and internet connection.

15. International Data Processing

Some third-party service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required, appropriate safeguards and legal protections will be applied to such processing.

16. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable.
  • Objecting to certain types of processing.
  • Managing advertising and privacy preferences.

Requests to access, correct, or delete personal information may be subject to applicable legal, security, verification, or operational requirements.

You may also be able to manage certain permissions through your device or platform settings.

17. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, remember preferences, and understand website usage.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Individual games may use different technologies depending on their platform.

18. External Links

Our website, games, or services may contain links to third-party websites and platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of external services.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, services, technology, or legal requirements change.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

20. Contact Information

If you have questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our official Contact page.

Contact Nishant GameDev Studios ↗

21. Applicable Laws

Our handling of personal information is intended to comply with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

Depending on where our users are located, different legal requirements may apply. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.

22. Final Notice

This Privacy Policy describes general information practices for Nishant GameDev Studios. The actual information collected and processed may vary depending on the game, platform, features enabled, and third-party services used.

Specific games may have additional notices explaining their individual data collection and processing practices.
